Task: Modeling lounge eligibility at Heathrow Terminal 3
https://www.theforage.com/virtual-experience/NjynCWzGSaWXQCxSX/british-airways/data-science-yqoz/modeling-lounge-eligibility-at-heathrow-terminal-3?step=1

Purpose: create a reusable lookup table of lounge eligibility percentages for future
flight schedules, supported by a written justification.

Use high-level categories, such as time of day, route type or destination region,
rather than depending on a particular flight number or aircraft. Estimate eligibility
for three tiers. The simulation's diagram defines:
  Tier 1, Concorde Room level: First Class, BA Premier, BA Gold Guest List.
  Tier 2, First Lounge: BA Gold.
  Tier 3, Club Lounge: BA Silver and Club World business class.

Apply the categories and rates to a representative sample of the provided schedule.
Analyzing every flight is not required. The illustrated sample includes flight number,
departure time, destination, category and three tier percentages.

The task explicitly treats Tier 1 as potential future capacity: it states that there
is currently no Concorde Room at Terminal 3. This is the exercise's planning scenario.
Eligibility estimates do not establish actual visits or simultaneous occupancy.

Required submission: the provided Excel lookup template with group percentages for
all three tiers, plus answers on its Justification worksheet explaining grouping,
rationale, assumptions and application to future/unknown schedules.
