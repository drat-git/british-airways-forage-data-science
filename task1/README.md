# Task 1: Lounge eligibility at Heathrow Terminal 3

The [working model](BA%20Lounge%20Eligibility%20Model.xlsx) contains three worksheets:

- **Schedule:** 10,000 flights with total-seat and category formulas.
- **Lookup:** eight groups, combining short/long haul with four times of day, and eligibility rates for three lounge tiers.
- **Sample:** application of the lookup to selected flights.

The [completed submission](submission/BA%20Lounge%20Eligibility%20Submission.xlsx) uses the official template and includes the lookup table plus four written justification answers. It is the preserved submitted workbook. Open either workbook in Excel or another compatible spreadsheet application.

Eligibility rates divide group total eligible passengers by group total seats. Seats proxy passengers under an explicit full-occupancy assumption. Rates generalise beyond individual flight numbers and aircraft, but do not establish actual attendance or peak lounge occupancy. Tier 1 represents hypothetical future provision in the exercise.

The [source schedule](data/British%20Airways%20Summer%20Schedule.xlsx), [blank template](resources/Lounge%20Eligibility%20Lookup%20Template.xlsx), and [assignment notes with original links](resources/assignment.txt) accompany the completed work.
